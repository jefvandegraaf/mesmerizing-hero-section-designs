# The designs

48 hero sections, polished and ready to use. Each one is a single HTML file.

- **See one:** open the file in your browser.
- **Make it yours:** change `--hl-acc`, `--hl-acc2` and `--i` near the top, then swap in your own headline and buttons.
- **Use it:** paste the `<style>`, the `<section>` and the `<script>` into your page, or hand the file to your AI.

Prefer clicking to coding? Pick your colours and headline on the [live gallery](https://jefvandegraaf.com/resources/hero-designs/), then copy the code.
